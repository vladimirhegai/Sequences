# Sequences for Slack — implementation history

This archive consolidates the dated implementation ledger that previously
lived in `CLAUDE.md` and `ROADMAP.md`. It is retained for incident archaeology,
not as current instructions. Use [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) for working rules,
[ROADMAP.md](../../ROADMAP.md) for live work, and
[SENTINEL.md](../../SENTINEL.md) for correctness ownership and fallback
diagnostics.

Detailed evidence remains in [PROBE_LOG.md](../../PROBE_LOG.md), the archived
[Sentinel report](SENTINEL_REPORT.md), the archived
[motion-design plan](MOTION_DESIGN_PLAN.md), and the Recipe Studio reports in
this directory.

## 2026-07-01 — direct authoring, executable cuts, and cinematography

- Direct HyperFrames HTML became the primary authored artifact. The host kept
  source validation, checkpoints, previews, rendering, and Slack delivery.
- Per-job `frame.md` art direction, embedded type systems, brand-token repair,
  and the host-owned cinematography kit established the visual baseline.
- Typed cut declarations began compiling through a local seek-safe runtime.
  The model-free Slack ad became the deterministic cut/render proof.
- The first paid OpenRouter smoke proved staged planning, typed-cut selection,
  kit injection, bounded repair, thumbnails, and MP4 output.

## 2026-07-02 — spatial world, components, and storyboard moments

- The continuous spatial-world camera contract added named stations, typed
  moves, gap-filling drift, depth layers, and deterministic live measurement.
- The motion-native component catalog added typed state beats and host-compiled
  internal motion, including live-geometry morph twins.
- Storyboard moments became the review contract: every promised changed state
  binds to executable evidence and drives the Slack outline and thumbnails.
- The Relay incident exposed silent safe-fallback publication. Named stage
  receipts and honest fallback labeling replaced that ambiguity.

## 2026-07-03 — shape matching, camera depth, and operator visibility

- Shape-match cuts gained measured bridge geometry, typed degradation, and
  plan-time requirement handling. Orbit and rack-focus depth followed.
- ETA countdowns, `/sequences debug`, stage attempt receipts, and reasoning
  controls made long runs observable.
- World-layout cells gave camera stations deterministic placement instead of
  freehand wall geometry.

## 2026-07-04 — time warps, browser truth, and reliability

- The time-ramp contract added net-zero, seek-safe viewer-time warping.
- Deterministic cut discovery upgraded one measured silhouette rhyme and
  persisted the mutated storyboard through every downstream artifact.
- Browser QA expanded to camera-arrival framing, exact temporal moment evidence,
  eye trace, sparse-frame coverage, and interaction-time truth.
- Source-author reliability gained persisted failed attempts, DOM-level kit
  audits, binding reconciliation, runtime-bind escalation, and strategy
  switching when compact repair did not converge.
- Pacing audits added camera ceilings, reading floors, outcome holds, and
  introduction/development discipline.

## 2026-07-05 — exit discipline and correctness ownership

- Exit, transition-coherence, and honest-thumbnail passes closed recurring
  overlap, cut-zoo, and empty-capture classes.
- Shared planning cache, source rescue, scene timing repair, moment top-up,
  unsupported-beat degradation, and component-markup completion reduced
  mechanically wasted paid attempts.
- Fail-loud diagnostics began writing `FAILURE.md` with named stage receipts
  and persisted attempt artifacts.
- Sentinel was designed: mechanically decidable obligations move to the lowest
  possible layer (schema, scaffold, normalize, static gate, browser gate, then
  paid retry). The original design and audit remain archived in this directory;
  [SENTINEL_REPORT.md](SENTINEL_REPORT.md) is the evidence ledger and the current contract is
  [SENTINEL.md](../../SENTINEL.md).

## 2026-07-06 — Sentinel default-on and motion texture

- Host skeletons and scene-addressable authoring slots became default-on, with
  scene-scoped repair and atomic storyboard normalization.
- The closed-world Sentinel registry, telemetry report, prompt budgets, and
  degradation honesty became CI-backed contracts.
- Host-derived motion texture shipped: the compact cut vocabulary, dive,
  sweeps/glows/draw-ons/echoes, headline letter machinery, animated grade
  shifts, and compact pop/stamp motion.

## 2026-07-07 — recipes, Studio pivot, and attempt economy

- RecipeV2 introduced proven, parameterized fragments that the host injects
  verbatim through the same production gate.
- Sequences Studio evolved into an operator-local viewer and gate/export UI.
  The removed canvas-builder and in-studio chat experiments remain documented
  only in the archived Recipe Studio reports.
- Scene-scoped storyboard and source repair, stagnant-polish early shipping,
  component trimming, framing top-up, and camera-energy lift cut repeated
  whole-document retries without loosening gates.

## 2026-07-08 — plugins, critic economy, and render polish

- Parameterized host plugins lowered seeded content into ordinary typed
  components and beats, so existing budgets and QA judged the executed plan.
- Critic directives that named scenes began using scene-slot repair under a
  strict non-regression guard.
- HD supersampling rendered a 2× master and Lanczos-downscaled it to remove
  sub-pixel stair-stepping from captured MP4s.
- Probe-driven runtime polish fixed no-op swaps, early swaps, connector spam,
  single-active navigation, generic topped-up labels, double-owned text
  entrances, dive harshness, and cross-family morph smearing.

## 2026-07-09 — assets and plugin-seam hardening

- Thirteen host-owned parametric assets, spring motion, `/sequences asset`, and
  the Studio Assets viewer shipped on plugin rails.
- Plugin entrances became camera-arrival-aware; injected units defended their
  placement; authored duplicates were hidden deterministically.
- Brand-base injection, finite-repeat repair, station positioning, generated
  world-layout cells, terminal wrapping, and centered station defaults removed
  recurring paid-attempt killers.
- Continuous direction and rendered motion evidence began persisting focal,
  velocity, acceleration, jerk, reversal, competition, and settle data.

## 2026-07-10 — continuity director and motion-quality architecture

- The Continuity Graph and Camera Blocking Director proved stable entity
  identities, measured cross-shot handoffs, graph-owned minimum-jerk routes,
  explicit occupancy/anchor/dwell paperwork, and temporal blocking overlays.
- Continuity/camera ownership moved to default-on with `=0` rollback after
  exact-project A/B and distinct paid SaaS proofs.
- The motion-quality closeout added host-owned environments and living canvas,
  occupancy-grid framing, station-size repair, whole-frame composition evidence,
  rendered dead-frame and outgoing-transition evidence, component follow chains
  and entrance families, washout/display-type discipline, semantic topology and
  comparison/pricing plugins, high-confidence recipe auto-declaration, shared
  host-contract metadata, a closed-world feature-flag inventory, and separate
  unit/browser test projects.
- The bounded critic gained native representative-strip and primary-blocking
  PNG inputs under an independent kill switch and the existing full-QA guard.
  Three choreography recipes were green-gated and exported for ambient hero,
  overlapping dashboard entrance, and outgoing morph-seam patterns.
